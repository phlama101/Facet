## Learning Objectives

1. Distinguish physical (mechanical) from chemical weathering and identify the main processes driving each.
2. Explain how rock type and climate together control which weathering processes dominate and at what rate.
3. Identify common weathering and erosion landforms: tors, hoodoos, arches, talus slopes, karst topography.

---

## Scope Boundaries

**Covers:** Physical weathering (frost action, thermal expansion, pressure release, biological), chemical weathering (dissolution, hydrolysis, oxidation), climate and rock-type controls, mass wasting, characteristic landforms.
**Does not cover:** River transport and deposition (1.5.3), glacial erosion (1.5.4), soil formation in depth.

## Section Outline

### Section 1 — Intro: "The invisible sculptor"
**Hook:** Arches National Park contains over 2,000 natural sandstone arches — not carved by rivers or human hands, but by ice expanding in cracks and by slightly acidic water dissolving the cement holding sand grains together. Key terms: **mechanical weathering**, **chemical weathering**, **erosion**, **mass wasting**, **karst**.

### Section 2 — Concept: "Breaking rock, shaping landscapes"
- Physical weathering: frost wedging (water expands 9% on freezing), thermal expansion, pressure release/exfoliation domes, salt crystallisation, biological (tree roots, lichens)
- Chemical weathering: dissolution (limestone in carbonic acid → karst), hydrolysis (feldspar → clay minerals), oxidation (iron minerals → rust-red soils and rock)
- Climate controls: cold/wet → frost dominant; hot/wet → chemical dominant; hot/dry → minimal chemical, physical dominant
- Rock-type controls: limestone dissolves readily; granite resists; sandstone weathers by cement dissolution; basalt weathers chemically to red tropical soils
- Mass wasting: rockfall, landslides, debris flows, creep
- Landforms: exfoliation domes (Yosemite's Half Dome), tors (Dartmoor), hoodoos (Bryce Canyon), natural arches (Arches NP), talus/scree slopes, karst (sinkholes, caves, limestone towers)
- Cards: Physical Weathering (Layers, BRAND.gold), Chemical Weathering & Karst (Waves, BRAND.accent), Erosion Landforms & Mass Wasting (Mountain, BRAND.coral)

### Section 3 — Quiz: 3 Questions
1. Frost wedging vs dissolution — which dominates where and why?
2. Why does granite weather to rounded boulders (tors) while limestone dissolves to create caves and sinkholes?
3. What is mass wasting and how does it shape slopes?
